# Cold email templates library

Source library: the top 30+ best-performing cold email templates used for client
lead generation, plus signal-driven workflow ideas (RB2B visitor triggers, competitor
follower scraping, and similar).

Used by the template gate in `stage-6-copywriting/campaign-copywriting/SKILL.md`.
Before any email copy is written, the agent recommends 3-4 templates from
`cold-email-templates-library.md` and shows what each would sound like for the user's
company.
